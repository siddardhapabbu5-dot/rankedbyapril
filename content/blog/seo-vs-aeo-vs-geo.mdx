Search still starts with intent. What changed is **where the answer appears**.

Buyers still Google. They also ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. Google itself now surfaces AI Overviews above many classic results. If your visibility strategy only targets blue links, you are optimizing for a shrinking share of attention.

## SEO still matters

Search Engine Optimization remains the foundation: crawlability, relevance, authority, and pages that match what people want. Without that base, AEO and GEO have nothing durable to stand on.

## AEO: own the answer

Answer Engine Optimization structures content so answer engines can lift a clear, verifiable response. Think:

- One primary question per section
- Direct answer in the first sentences
- Supporting proof, not fluff
- FAQ and schema that reinforce the answer

## GEO: get named and cited

Generative Engine Optimization is about becoming a source models trust enough to cite or recommend. Entity consistency, third-party corroboration, and quotable writing all matter.

## What to do this quarter

1. Audit where you appear in AI answers today
2. Rewrite money pages for answer-first structure
3. Strengthen schema, authorship, and brand entity signals
4. Track citations the way you track rankings

Nobody can guarantee an AI citation. You *can* make your brand eligible — structured, credible, and easy to quote.
