Most content fails before the first draft — because the brief was vague.

## Brief essentials

1. Primary query and close variants
2. Search intent and SERP format notes
3. Must-cover entities and questions
4. Unique angle vs. competing pages
5. Internal links to include
6. Conversion goal for the page

## Answer-first outlines

Put the clearest answer near the top. Support it with depth. End sections with a natural next step — not a hard sell in every paragraph.

## AI drafts still need humans

Use AI to accelerate research and first drafts. Use editors for voice, accuracy, and E-E-A-T. Publish only what you'd put your name on.

A strong brief makes average writers good — and good writers excellent.
