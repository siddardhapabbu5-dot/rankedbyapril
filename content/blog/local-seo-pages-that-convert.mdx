Multi-location brands often ship the same paragraph with the city name swapped. Google notices. Customers notice. Conversions suffer.

## What unique actually means

Unique is not synonym-spinning. It means:

- Local proof (reviews, photos, team, service nuances)
- Neighborhood and service-area specifics
- Distinct FAQs people ask in that market
- Clear calls to action tied to that location

## Pair pages with GBP

Your Google Business Profile and location page should reinforce the same NAP, categories, and services. Reviews and posts should feed both trust systems.

## Measure what matters

Track local pack presence, calls, direction requests, and form submits by location — not just keyword rankings in a spreadsheet.

Build a template. Then force every location to earn its uniqueness before publish.
